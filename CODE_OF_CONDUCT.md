# Community conduct

Treat participants with respect. Discuss technical decisions with evidence. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove violating content and restrict participation. Conduct concerns can be raised privately with a SigmaUno organization administrator using their published contact channels. Do not expose private incident details in public issues. Maintainers should explain enforcement decisions to affected participants where safe and consider appeals through another organization administrator when available.
