# European regulatory context

Design context, not a certification or a deployment-specific legal opinion. Reviewed sources: 11 September 2026.

Regulation (EU) 2024/1183 adds electronic-ledger provisions to eIDAS. Article 45k distinguishes admissibility of electronic ledgers from the presumptions attached to records in qualified electronic ledgers: unique and accurate sequential chronological ordering and integrity. Qualification is not inferred from consensus technology. [Official text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1183/oj/eng).

Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2531 sets reference standards and specifications for qualified electronic ledgers. It does not make any public blockchain automatically qualified. [Official text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2025/2531/oj/eng).

Qualified timestamps, seals, electronic ledgers, and archiving services supply distinct assertions. Verify the specific provider/service qualification and relevant historical status, certificate evidence, policy, and validation requirements before reporting a qualified result.

Future profiles such as SP-EU-GENERAL-1, SP-EU-EIDAS-1, SP-IT-DOCS-1, and SP-IT-FINANCE-1 are proposed names only. They need versioned machine-readable definitions, human explanation, signatures, audit history, and review by specialists in eIDAS, Italian CAD, AgID document management, conservation, and digital signatures. A profile cannot establish complete organizational compliance merely by counting anchors.
